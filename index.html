3. What to notice

Smooth ease starts gently, speeds up, then settles. It is useful when you want motion to feel designed rather than mechanical.

Try it in Alight Motion: add a keyframe for a layer's position near the start, move the playhead, change the position and add another keyframe. Then open the easing controls and compare curves. Button labels can vary by app version.
